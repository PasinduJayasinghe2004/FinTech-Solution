Design a modern, premium, and professional SaaS landing page for a web platform called "RIA".

RIA is a teacher-centric tuition payment and student monitoring platform designed for private tuition teachers. The platform helps teachers manage students, track monthly tuition payments, monitor outstanding balances, receive payment notifications, and view financial analytics from one centralized dashboard.

TARGET USERS:
Private tuition teachers and educational instructors who manage multiple students and need a simple digital system for student payments and monitoring.

DESIGN STYLE:
Create a modern FinTech + EdTech SaaS interface.
The design should feel trustworthy, secure, clean, premium, and easy to use.

Use:
- Modern minimal UI
- Professional SaaS design
- Soft gradients
- Rounded cards
- Clean spacing
- Subtle shadows
- Premium typography
- Modern dashboard illustrations
- Smooth visual hierarchy

COLOR PALETTE:
Primary: Deep Blue
Secondary: Teal / Emerald Green
Background: White and very light blue-gray
Accent: Soft green for successful payments
Warning: Orange for pending payments
Danger: Soft red for overdue payments

Avoid excessive colors. Keep the design elegant and professional.

LANDING PAGE STRUCTURE:

1. NAVIGATION BAR
Include:
- RIA logo
- Home
- Features
- How It Works
- Pricing
- About Us
- Login button
- Get Started button

Make the navbar clean and sticky.

2. HERO SECTION

Main headline:
"Manage Your Tuition Payments. Without the Stress."

Supporting text:
"RIA helps teachers manage students, track payments, monitor outstanding balances, and receive real-time updates — all in one platform."

Include two CTA buttons:
- Get Started Free
- Watch Demo

On the right side, display a beautiful modern teacher dashboard mockup showing:
- Total Students
- Monthly Revenue
- Pending Payments
- Outstanding Balance
- Payment Analytics Chart

Add subtle floating UI cards around the dashboard such as:
- Payment Received
- New Student Added
- Outstanding Payment Reminder

3. TRUST / SOCIAL PROOF SECTION

Display a short message:
"Everything a modern tuition teacher needs to manage payments."

Include simple statistics:
- 500+ Teachers
- 10,000+ Students
- Rs. 10M+ Payments Tracked

Use clean icon-based cards.

4. PROBLEM SECTION

Headline:
"Managing Tuition Payments Shouldn't Be Complicated."

Show common problems teachers face:

- Tracking payments manually
- Forgetting who has paid
- Managing outstanding balances
- Sending payment reminders manually
- Using spreadsheets and notebooks

Use a modern visual comparison between traditional manual management and the RIA platform.

5. FEATURES SECTION

Headline:
"Everything You Need to Manage Your Tuition Business"

Create beautiful feature cards for:

Student Management
"Manage all your students from one place with unique student IDs."

Smart Payment Tracking
"Track paid, pending, and overdue payments in real time."

Real-Time Dashboard
"Get a complete overview of your students and finances."

Secure Payments
"Provide secure and reliable digital payment options."

Automatic Notifications
"Automatically notify students about payments and reminders."

Analytics & Reports
"Understand your tuition business with powerful insights and reports."

Use modern line icons and subtle hover effects.

6. HOW IT WORKS SECTION

Create a simple 4-step visual flow:

Step 1:
Teacher creates their tuition profile.

Step 2:
Students receive their unique Student ID.

Step 3:
Students log in and complete their payment.

Step 4:
Teacher receives real-time payment updates.

Use connected cards or a horizontal timeline.

7. PRODUCT DASHBOARD SHOWCASE

Headline:
"Your Tuition Business. All in One Dashboard."

Display a large realistic dashboard interface.

Include:
- Total Students

Make this section visually impressive and premium.

8. PAYMENT & SECURITY SECTION

- Shield icon

The design should communicate trust and financial security.

9. NOTIFICATION SECTION

Headline:
"Never Miss a Payment Update."

Show mobile notification UI examples:

"Payment Successful"

10. CALL TO ACTION SECTION

Headline:
"Start Managing Your Tuition Business Smarter."

Supporting text:
"Join modern teachers who are simplifying student payments and tuition management."

Buttons:
- Get Started Free
- Book a Demo

Use a strong gradient background and visually attractive design.

11. FOOTER

Include:
- RIA logo
- Product links
- Features
- Pricing
- Help Center
- Contact
- Privacy Policy
- Terms & Conditions

Include social media icons.

RESPONSIVE DESIGN:
Create both desktop and mobile responsive layouts.

IMPORTANT UI/UX REQUIREMENTS:
- Follow modern SaaS UX principles
- Use a strong visual hierarchy
- Make CTAs highly visible
- Maintain generous whitespace
- Use a maximum content width for readability
- Ensure accessibility and readable contrast
- Design components consistently
- Use an 8px spacing system
- Use modern rounded cards
- Make the interface look like a real startup product ready for launch

The final design should feel similar in quality to a modern FinTech and SaaS startup, combining the trust of a banking product with the simplicity of an educational platform.
